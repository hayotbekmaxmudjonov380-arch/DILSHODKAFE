## 🎬 3. Motion Design, 3D Graphics & 4K Animation Engineering

To create a **mind-blowing interactive experience** on the web, follow these motion design physics and implementation rules:

### 1. 3D Perspective Tilt Physics (Hero & Cards)
* **Technique:** Real-time pointer tracking with smooth CSS 3D transform matrix:
  ```css
  perspective: 1000px;
  transform: rotateX(calc(var(--mouse-y) * -12deg)) 
             rotateY(calc(var(--mouse-x) * 12deg)) 
             scale3d(1.02, 1.02, 1.02);
  transition: transform 0.25s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1);
  ```
* **Effect:** When the user moves their cursor over the hero dish or menu items, the card tilts subtly in 3D space with an inner specular light reflection.

### 2. Smooth Transition Physics (Easing & Curves)
* **Standard Easing Token:** `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` (Apple/Framer standard spring curve for zero-jank responsiveness).
* **Hover Micro-Animations:**
  - Buttons expand with a subtle light sweep (`linear-gradient(60deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.25), transparent)`).
  - Floating badges utilize a gentle continuous oscillation `translateY(-8px)` over a 4-second sinusoidal loop.

### 3. Ambient 3D Depth Lighting (Glow Follower)
* Two radial gradient blurs (`filter: blur(140px)`) positioned at the top-right (Amber Gold `#E5A93C`) and bottom-left (Emerald `#10B981`) respond to global mouse coordinates with an inverse parallax multiplier `(x * 40px, y * 40px)`.

### 4. Interactive Tabs & Stagger Reveal
* When switching between "Milliy taomlar", "Fast-food", "Ichimliklar", and "Desertlar", items animate out with `opacity: 0; transform: translateY(16px)` and stagger back into view within 350ms for a crisp native-app feel.
